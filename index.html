About

Hi, I'm Rama.

Born in Palakkad and raised in Chennai, India, I moved to Coimbatore for my MSc in Physics at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham. There, I found myself drawn to research but wanted to apply my computational skills in a more focused domain which led me to Ahmedabad for a PGDip in Space and Atmospheric Science at the Physical Research Laboratory (PRL).

A course during my diploma sparked a lasting interest in the Moon's permanently shadowed regions (PSRs), prime targets for future lunar missions seeking water ice. My time at PRL deepened this interest further, as I learned how impact cratering could both disturbs subsurface ice and helps preserve it through ejected material. Since then, my research has centered on PSRs and impact craters in the Moon's south polar region. I'm also in projects involving Mars.

Research

Where the light doesn't reach, the questions begin.

Crater Mapping & Micro-PSRs

Sub-decameter crater mapping and micro-PSR analysis in the lunar south polar region using LRO NAC imagery and QuickMap, with CSFD analysis via Craterstats and CraterTools to refine volatile preservation estimates.

Impact Simulation

iSALE-2D forward modeling of impact cratering, working through equations of state (Tillotson, ANEOS) to connect simulated crater morphology to fault-controlled deformation.
